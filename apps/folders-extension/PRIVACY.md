# Folders for YouTube: privacy policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Folders for YouTube is a browser extension that organizes your YouTube
subscriptions and playlists into folders. It has no server, no account and no
analytics. The developer never receives any of your data.

## What the extension stores

- **Your folders and which channels and playlists are in them**, plus your
  settings. They are kept in your browser's extension storage and in your
  browser's own sync storage (Chrome, Edge or Firefox sync), so they follow you
  to your other computers if you have browser sync turned on.
- **A local copy of your subscription and playlist list** (titles, thumbnail
  addresses, ids) and recent videos from channels' public feeds, so the
  extension can show them quickly. These stay on your computer only.

You can remove everything by uninstalling the extension.

## What the extension sends, and where

All requests go to YouTube (`www.youtube.com`), from your own browser, using
the YouTube session you are already signed in with:

- reading your subscriptions and playlists, and the public feed of recent
  uploads for channels in your folders;
- when you ask for it and confirm: unsubscribing from channels, deleting
  playlists, and creating or adding videos to a playlist when you merge
  playlists.

Nothing is sent anywhere else. The extension does not read or transmit your
password or cookies beyond what YouTube's own page uses to authorize these
requests, and it never sells, shares or transfers data to third parties.
